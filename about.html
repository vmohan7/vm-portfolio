Full bio

Vasanth Mohan is Senior Director of Technical Product Marketing and Developer Relations at SambaNova. He writes and speaks about the hardware behind fast AI: where latency comes from, what it costs, and why coding agents need more than a capable model. His work on agentic AI and disaggregated inference explains how GPUs handle prefill while SambaNova's RDUs handle decoding.

At AI By the Bay and Hacking Agents, Vasanth has taken those tradeoffs onstage in talks about faster inference and new hardware for AI agents. He connects infrastructure choices to the thing developers feel first: how quickly an agent responds.

Before SambaNova, Vasanth helped developers build for the telecom edge at MobiledgeX. Through FusedVR, he taught VR development with Unity and SteamVR and co-authored O'Reilly's Creating Augmented and Virtual Realities with Erin Pangilinan and Steve Lukas. He has also joined an AWE USA panel on 5G edge computing for AR/VR and spoken on EnterVR about VR adoption. He is based in San Jose, California.
