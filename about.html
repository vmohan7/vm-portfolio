Head of Dev Rel & Product Marketing @ SambaNova | Agentic AI, Fast & Energy-Efficient Inference, Sovereign AI

Full bio

Vasanth Mohan is Head of Developer Relations and Product Marketing at SambaNova, where his work focuses on agentic AI, fast and energy-efficient inference, and sovereign AI. He writes and speaks about the infrastructure behind AI applications, including how hardware choices affect the speed, cost, and usability of coding agents and large language models. His recent writing explores disaggregated inference, with GPUs handling prefill and SambaNova's RDUs handling decode for AI-agent workloads.

His background includes developer advocacy at MobiledgeX, where he helped developers bring applications to the telecom edge, from early prototypes through production deployments. That work included the practical challenges of AR and VR infrastructure, including edge rendering and 5G connectivity.

Through FusedVR, Vasanth has shared hands-on guidance for building immersive applications with Unity and SteamVR. He is a co-author of Creating Augmented and Virtual Realities, published by O'Reilly in 2019 with Erin Pangilinan and Steve Lukas. His tutorials cover practical development problems such as managing scene transitions without disrupting the experience inside a headset.

Vasanth has given talks at AI By the Bay and Hacking Agents and joined an AWE USA panel on 5G edge computing for AR and VR. He has also appeared on EnterVR to discuss VR adoption and the metaverse. He is based in San Jose, California.
