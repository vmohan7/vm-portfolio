Short bio

Vasanth Mohan serves as Senior Director of Technical Product Marketing and Developer Relations at SambaNova. In previous roles, he has worked with thousands of developers to empower them to build the best scalable solutions across many emerging technologies, including VR/AR, Edge Computing, and AI. At SambaNova, he is now focused on enabling developers and enterprises to unlock the value of Agentic AI with Fast Inference on SambaCloud to solve today's business challenges quickly and efficiently.

Full bio

Vasanth Mohan built his early public work around immersive technology. Through FusedVR, he taught developers to build VR experiences with Unity and SteamVR, turning hands-on experiments into practical tutorials. He later co-authored O'Reilly's Creating Augmented and Virtual Realities with Erin Pangilinan and Steve Lukas.

His focus then shifted to the telecom edge. At MobiledgeX, Vasanth helped developers move applications from prototype to production, working at the intersection of AR/VR, 5G connectivity, and edge rendering. The question was practical: how do you make demanding experiences work beyond a demo?

Today, Vasanth is Senior Director of Technical Product Marketing and Developer Relations at SambaNova. He works with developers and enterprises on agentic AI, coding agents, and fast inference. The technology has changed, but the throughline is familiar: making powerful systems useful to the people building with them. He is based in San Jose, California.
